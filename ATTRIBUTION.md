# Attribution and provenance

## Origin

The stable method was developed while hardening and independently reviewing the public `gpt-thinking-block-mcp` project. The public case study identifies the exact upstream and hardened revisions used as evidence.

This repository is a newly authored engineering reference. It is not a copy of private continuity or review transcripts, and it does not treat a case-study repository's license as the license for this independent work.

## Source classes

- Normative protocol claims cite official MCP specifications, JSON-RPC 2.0, and IETF RFCs.
- Moving integration guidance carries an assessment date and links to the relevant official host or extension documentation.
- Case-study claims bind to public source revisions and sanitized reproduction receipts.
- Private raw commands, stdout/stderr, review transcripts, and local paths remain outside this repository.

## Model contributions

Codex contributed implementation, testing, synthesis, and editorial work. GPT-5.6 Pro contributed independent review and methodological critique. Neither model identity, elapsed reasoning time, test name, nor review confidence is used as an assurance level.

## Architecture atlas

Faye selected the architecture publication scope, bilingual sibling contract,
and final visual direction. Codex modeled and rendered the atlas from the
repository's public source, release, case-study, and evaluation evidence. The
model and figures are explanatory reconstructions: they preserve named owners,
versions, evidence ceilings, feedback routes, and declared unknowns, but they
do not create runtime, host, adoption, or independent-assurance evidence.
